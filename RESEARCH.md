# Five-person usability study — not yet conducted

Recruit 3 people who reconcile invoices or handle payment support and 2 product/engineering peers. Ask permission to record observations and publish anonymized quotes; do not collect customer financial records.

## Tasks
1. Explain why Cedar Workshop needs action at 24h but not 72h.
2. Decide whether Juniper Supply received two payments.
3. Investigate Common Ground and choose the next step.
4. Record a decision, then explain whether the invoice is resolved.
5. Import a sample, find an exception and export the review.

## Record
Task completion; correct/incorrect decision; time; points of hesitation; exact words with consent; confidence from 1–5; requested changes. Do not coach until a participant is stuck. Do not use a five-person study to claim a percentage improvement in business outcomes.

## Decision rules
Revise any label that leads to a harmful action in even one session. Investigate patterns affecting two or more participants. After changes, retest action choice and whether participants understand approval versus resolution. Report limitations and contradictory feedback.
