    02 / WHY IT WORKS THIS WAY

    Automation needs a stopping point.

    01

    A duplicate is not a second payment.

    Matching event IDs are counted once. Conflicting payloads and distinct IDs that exceed an invoice are routed for review.

    02

    Wait before calling it broken.

    A configurable grace window separates expected sync delay from overdue reconciliation. Change it and see the queue move.

    03

    Approval is not resolution.

    An operator can record a reasoned next step. The exception stays visible until new source evidence actually resolves it.

    The next test is with people.

    The current proof is a working rules engine and automated edge-case tests. A five-person study should test whether operators choose the right action and can explain why. No customer adoption or business lift is claimed.
